# Business Requirements Specification – CO2Store

| ID | Requirement |
|----|-------------|
| BR-1 | Check whether the reported capacity of a storage unit follows from its reported geometry and rock/fluid parameters. |
| BR-2 | Predict log10 capacity for a unit from geology (without the storage-efficiency factor) and give a calibrated interval. |
| BR-3 | Report the interval's coverage when the unit comes from a country not seen in training. |
| BR-4 | State that all capacities are estimates, not injectivity or containment evidence. |

Out of scope: dynamic simulation, injectivity, containment risk, economics, site selection.
